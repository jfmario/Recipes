# Kung Pao Chicken

This Kung Pao Chicken is a fast, high-heat stir fry with bold takeout-style flavor: savory soy, bright vinegar, balanced sweetness, and the signature tingle of Sichuan pepper.

For the best texture, have everything measured and prepped before the wok goes on the heat. The full batch cooks in one pass in a large wok or deep skillet.

## Ingredients

* 2 lb (1 kg) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
* 1 tbsp + 1 tsp cornstarch
* 2 tbsp light soy sauce
* 3 tbsp dark soy sauce
* 1/4 cup Chinese black vinegar
* 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
* 6 tbsp sugar
* 1 tsp sesame oil
* 2/3 cup water
* 1/4 cup peanut oil (or other neutral high-heat oil)
* 4 garlic cloves
* 2 tsp fresh ginger
* 12 to 16 dried red chilies
* 6 green onions
* 1 tbsp ground Sichuan peppercorns, plus more to taste
* 1 1/2 cups roasted unsalted peanuts

## Steps

* If serving with rice, start it first so it is ready when the stir fry finishes.
* Cut 2 lb (1 kg) boneless, skinless chicken thighs into bite-size pieces.
* Mince 4 garlic cloves. Finely chop 2 tsp fresh ginger. Cut 12 to 16 dried red chilies into short pieces, discarding most seeds. Cut 6 green onions into 3/4-inch pieces, keeping the white and green parts in separate piles.
* Place the cut chicken in a bowl. In a second bowl, whisk 1 tbsp + 1 tsp cornstarch with 2 tbsp light soy sauce until smooth, then whisk in 3 tbsp dark soy sauce, 1/4 cup Chinese black vinegar, 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 6 tbsp sugar, and 1 tsp sesame oil.
* Pour 3 tbsp of the sauce mixture over the chicken and toss well. Let the chicken marinate for 15 to 20 minutes at room temperature. Whisk 2/3 cup water into the remaining sauce and set it beside the stove.
* Heat a wok or large skillet over high heat until very hot. Add 1/4 cup peanut oil, then add the minced 4 garlic cloves, 2 tsp chopped ginger, and prepared dried chilies. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
* Add all of the marinated chicken and the white parts from the 6 green onions. Stir-fry over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes, until the chicken is nearly cooked through.
* Re-whisk the sauce, then pour in all of it and add 1 tbsp ground Sichuan peppercorns. Stir constantly for 1 to 2 minutes, until the sauce reduces to a thick, glossy syrup that coats the chicken.
* Add 1 1/2 cups peanuts and the green parts from the 6 green onions. Toss for 20 to 30 seconds until heated through.
* Taste and add an extra pinch of ground Sichuan peppercorns if you want more numbing heat. Serve immediately.
