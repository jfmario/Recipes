# Tuna Pasta Sandwich

This quick solo meal is scaled for 1 serving rather than the usual 8. It uses a small amount of pasta to make the tuna filling more substantial without turning it into a full pasta salad.

## Ingredients

* 2 slices sandwich bread
* 1 (4 oz) can tuna packed in water, drained well
* 1/3 cup dry small pasta, such as elbows, shells, or ditalini
* 1 celery rib
* 1 dill pickle spear
* 1 tbsp mayonnaise
* 1 tbsp plain Greek yogurt or sour cream
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard
* 1 tsp lemon juice
* 1 lettuce leaf

## Steps

* Bring a small pot of salted water to a boil over high heat.
* Add 1/3 cup dry pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente, usually 7 to 10 minutes.
* While the pasta cooks, finely dice 1 celery rib and 1 dill pickle spear.
* Drain the pasta, rinse briefly under cold water until cool, shake off excess water, and transfer it to a small mixing bowl.
* Add the drained 1 (4 oz) can tuna, diced celery, diced pickle, 1 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp Greek yogurt or sour cream, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp lemon juice, a pinch of kosher salt, and a pinch of black pepper.
* Stir until the tuna breaks up and the pasta is evenly coated. Taste and adjust with a little more salt, pepper, or lemon juice if needed.
* Toast 2 slices sandwich bread if you want extra structure.
* Build the sandwich with 1 lettuce leaf on one slice of bread, pile on the tuna pasta filling, close with the second slice, and serve right away.
